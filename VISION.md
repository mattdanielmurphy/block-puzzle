# Don Blocko: vision

*Started 2026-11-27 from your notes. Nothing here is built yet.*

## Two games under one roof

- **Classic** stays exactly as the game is today, picked from the main menu. You: "I kind of love the game as it is, and I want to preserve the 'purity' of the main arcade game." The leaderboard stays Classic-only.
- **The campaign, "Custody of Nine"** (also the App Store subtitle), is where the game grows. You: arcade games suffer from "doing the SAME basic thing again and again"; the campaign should progress and keep people playing.

**Take:** right call. Keeping Classic untouched protects what already works and keeps the leaderboard fair, and the campaign gets room to be weird. The risk is size: a campaign with cinematics is months of work. Start with three short stages and one title card each, ship that, and see whether people play past stage 1.

## The campaign

- **Stages with their own rules,** each a beat of Don's story (the meeting, the house, the custody hearing…). Different game modes live inside it, not as separate menu items.
- **Stage goals beyond "score":** e.g. clean the house (see the capacity gauge in IDEAS.md), clear with only certain block types, beat Linda's score.
- **"Nine" ties it together:** the 9×9 grid, the nine boxes, maybe nine stages.

**Take:** stage goals are what make this a campaign and not just Classic with a story. Each new rule (directional blocks, the gauge) should be introduced by one stage, then mixed into later ones, the way puzzle games teach a mechanic and then combine them.
