# Don Blocko privacy policy

*Last updated 26 November 2026.*

Don Blocko is a block puzzle game. It has no accounts, no ads and no in-app purchases, and it does not sell or share your data.

## What stays on your device

Your game in progress, best scores, settings and daily play-limit time are stored only on your device.

## What reaches our server

Only if you choose to put a score on the public leaderboard:

- **The name you type** and your score, which everyone can see on the leaderboard. Offensive names are refused.
- **A replay of your moves**, used only to check the score is real.
- **Your IP address and device type** (browser user agent), used only to recognise the same player when they come back, so your name and best score follow you, and to stop spam. They are never shown to other players.

The web version also uses [Vercel Web Analytics](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy), which counts page visits without cookies and without identifying you.

## What we don't do

No tracking across other apps or websites, no advertising, no selling or sharing data with anyone.

## Removing your data

Use the feedback button in TestFlight (or reply to the beta invitation) to ask for your leaderboard name and scores to be removed, and they will be deleted.
